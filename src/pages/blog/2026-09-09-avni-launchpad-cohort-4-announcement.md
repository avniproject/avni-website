#### Announcing Avni Launchpad 4.0 – West and East India Cohort

After three cohorts of Avni Launchpad, we are excited to bring the next edition of the program to two regional hubs, Ahmedabad and Bhubaneswar, with a special focus on organisations working across Western and Eastern India.

Avni Launchpad 4.0 aims to bring together NGOs, social enterprises, CSR and funding organisations, and technology consultants from across West and East India to collaboratively solve field implementation and program monitoring challenges using Avni.

The earlier cohorts of Avni Launchpad brought together a diverse group of forward-thinking organisations looking to strengthen the way they manage their field programs. Over just a few weeks, participating organisations designed and launched digital pilots using Avni across sectors such as health, agriculture, education, livelihoods and social protection.

These pilots helped teams move from paper-based processes and spreadsheets to digital systems for data collection, beneficiary tracking, monitoring, reporting and real-time program insights.

Building on these experiences, the West and East India Cohort is an opportunity to work more closely with organisations across both regions, including those serving rural, remote and low-connectivity communities, contexts for which Avni has been specifically designed.

Many organisations running field-based programs still rely on cumbersome paper-based processes, spreadsheets or expensive enterprise systems. Even when organisations know they need a better digital system, taking the first step can be difficult: teams need to understand what to digitise, how the system should work, whether their staff will be able to use it, and whether the investment will create enough value.

Avni addresses these challenges through an open-source, low-code and AI-supported platform that enables organisations to build offline-capable field applications and access timely program information.

  <p style="margin: 0 0 12px 0; font-size: 1.15rem; font-weight: 700; line-height: 1.6;">Avni Launchpad makes it easier for organisations to take that first step.</p>
  <p style="margin: 0; line-height: 1.8;">Instead of making a major technology investment upfront, selected organisations work with the Avni team to design, build and test a real digital pilot for one of their own programs before deciding whether to scale it further.</p>

The Launchpad is a cohort-based program for NGOs, social enterprises, CSR teams, funders and technology consultants who want to strengthen social impact programs through better digital systems. Over a few weeks, selected participants receive hands-on mentoring, implementation guidance and access to the Avni platform to build a working pilot suited to their own field realities.

### What Does Avni Launchpad Cost?

Once you apply for the cohort, we will schedule a screening call to understand your requirements and assess whether Avni is a good fit for your use case.

If a suitable use case is identified and your organisation is selected for Launchpad Cohort 4, you will be invited to confirm your participation. Once you confirm, a one-time participation fee of **₹3,000** will apply.

As part of the cohort, participating organisations will receive access to the Avni platform, pilot hosting, mentoring, implementation guidance, training, and support to build and test one real use case.

There is no obligation to continue using Avni after the Launchpad. At the end of the pilot, your organisation can evaluate whether Avni meets your needs and decide whether you would like to continue. Continued use of Avni after the Launchpad is paid; please refer to the [Avni pricing page](https://avniproject.org/pricing/) for details.

  <p style="margin: 0 0 10px 0; line-height: 1.8;"><strong>Workshop travel and accommodation:</strong> Participating organisations will need to bear the travel and accommodation costs for their representative(s) attending the 2-day in-person workshop in Ahmedabad or Bhubaneswar.</p>
  <p style="margin: 0; line-height: 1.8;">Selected organisations may nominate one or two participants to attend the in-person workshop, depending on what is most useful for their pilot.</p>

### Who Should Apply?

Avni is best suited for organisations running field-based programs where services are delivered over time through community workers, field staff, volunteers or frontline teams.

The platform is particularly useful when programs require:

<div style="padding: 24px 28px; margin: 20px 0; background: #ffffff; border: 1px solid #e5e7eb; border-radius: 8px;">
<ul style="margin: 0; margin-left: 20px; line-height: 1.9; column-width: 240px; column-gap: 40px;">
    <li>regular field data collection;</li>
    <li>beneficiary or household tracking;</li>
    <li>follow-up over time;</li>
    <li>workflows for service delivery;</li>
    <li>monitoring of activities and outcomes;</li>
    <li>offline data collection;</li>
    <li>dashboards and reporting; or</li>
    <li>coordination across field teams.</li>
</ul>
</div>

The program can also be relevant for CSR teams and funding organisations looking to strengthen monitoring and data systems across partner organisations, as well as technology consultants and implementation partners interested in developing Program MIS and workflow-driven applications for the social sector using an open-source, low-code platform.

Launchpad works best when an organisation already has a specific program or use case that it wants to digitise and is prepared to actively work on the pilot during the cohort.
