Picture a clinic in a van, parked in a village with no signal. The receptionist registers a patient on her phone. A minute later the patient is on the nurse's phone. Nobody needs to press a button.

That is camp mode, new in Avni 18.2, and it runs on the oldest invention we have. One phone becomes the hub. It starts its own Wi-Fi network, and the other phones join it. They are the spokes.

Medical vans are where it started, but nothing in it is medical. A legal aid camp, a survey camp, any day where several people record on their own phones with no internet, works the same way.

## The wheel : on one screen

| On the wheel | What it means |
|---|---|
| The hub | One phone in the clinic. It starts its own Wi-Fi network |
| The spokes | Each joined phone shares with the hub every 30 seconds by itself |
| The road | The server. After a phone leaves the camp, it syncs with the server as usual |
| A camp | Starting a hub, or joining one, puts a phone in a camp. A phone in a camp does not sync with the server |
| What you get | The staff work on their own phones as one team, without internet |
| Is it ready? | Shipped in 18.2, and a pilot is under way |

## Why : one patient, four phones

Avni grew up with a field worker walking alone from village to village. Her phone keeps her work, and the server is where it meets everyone else's. We call that meeting a sync. Until now the server was the only place two phones could meet.

In a van with four people on four phones and no signal, the receptionist's new patient stayed on her phone. The nurse could wait for the evening, or register her twice. Paper starts to look good.

Several organisations running mobile clinics asked us to let the phones in the van talk to each other. We first tried every phone talking to every other, which proved fragile across makes of phone. Then a box in the van, which meant building a second app for the staff. So we reinvented the wheel, on purpose: one of the phones becomes the hub.

## A day in camp : one patient, once round the wheel

At eight the receptionist opens **Clinic sharing** on the hub phone. She taps **Start hub**.

![Start hub](/img/camp-mode/start-hub.png)
*Make this phone the hub. Somebody has to.*

Her screen shows a code to scan, with the name and password of its Wi-Fi network.

![A running hub](/img/camp-mode/running-hub.png)
*The hub, counting heads: phones synced in the last 5 minutes.*

The other three in the van tap **Scan to join**. They point their cameras at the code on the hub.

![A joined phone](/img/camp-mode/joined-phone.png)
*A spoke. Sync with hub is there for the impatient.*

Our patient arrives at nine, and the receptionist registers her on the hub. A minute later our patient is on the nurse's phone. The nurse records her vitals, and the doctor sees them on his phone. The nurse adds a photo too, and it travels to the hub with her visit. When he opens the photo, his phone fetches it from the hub.

At eleven the nurse walks out of range, and her phone loses the hub's network. Her screen offers **Rejoin** with the hub's name. She taps it when she is back.

At two the hub phone restarts, and the hub starts again by itself when the receptionist opens Avni. It makes a new network with a new name and password, so the other three walk over and scan its new code. Records shared before the restart stay on their phones.

At five the receptionist taps **End camp**. The hub asks her to tap it again to confirm. "This cannot be undone," it warns.

![End camp](/img/camp-mode/end-camp.png)
*The last question of the day.*

Her colleagues keep Avni open. At its next sync with the hub, each of their phones syncs one last time, then leaves the camp. The hub ticks off each phone as it leaves. Once all three have left, she taps **Close hub**. The hub deletes the records and photos it kept for the other phones.

The van drives back into signal. After leaving the camp, each phone syncs with the server as usual, and uploads its own work. The nurse's visit waits on her phone until our patient, registered on the receptionist's phone, reaches the server.

That is the catch. Every phone needs a sync once it is back in signal.

## Before you roll : a switch and some house rules

1. In the web app, open the organisation settings page. Turn on **Enable clinic sharing**. With on-device data encryption on, the **Clinic sharing** menu stays hidden.
2. Sync every phone with the server just before the camp, so that its forms and other organisation setup match the hub's.
3. The hub phone needs Android 10 or later. Its user needs full access, with all privileges.
4. Each person should edit only the records they created. Through the hub, the version that reaches the hub last is kept.

![Enable clinic sharing](/img/camp-mode/org-setting.png)
*Turn on Enable clinic sharing.*

The [clinic sharing guide](https://avni.readme.io/docs/clinic-sharing) has the rest.

## Under the hub cap : for the technically curious

Photos travel to the hub with their records. A joined phone syncs with the hub every 30 seconds while Avni is open.

On the server, the last upload wins, as with sync.

A record from another phone belongs to that phone. That phone uploads it to the server. Until it does, this phone holds back its own work that depends on it.
